How I work

Three commitments

Start from the decision

Every project here begins with a question someone had to answer, not with a dataset that happened to exist.

Separate signal from cause

A metric shows where a problem surfaces. Naming the cause takes a hypothesis and a test designed to be able to fail.

Claim only what the evidence supports

Measured results and proposed frameworks are labeled separately throughout. Nothing here is dressed up as more than it is.
